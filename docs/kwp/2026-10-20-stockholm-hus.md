## What this tells us

1. **Stockholm's share of Sweden.** Stockholm accounts for about 30–40 % of national volume on the core house terms: 210 of 720, 210 of 590 and 260 of 720.
2. **Price searches are growing fastest.** They are up 50–150 % on three months, and "vad kostar en husbesiktning" is also +55 % year on year. That supports `/husbesiktning/pris/`.
3. **Negative year-on-year change is seasonality.** The comparison is made in autumn; searches peak in spring (March). Pages should be indexed before February.
4. **Within the county, only Värmdö has its own volume** (90). It is mentioned in a section rather than given its own page.
5. **For Google Ads,** price phrases have medium or low competition and cheaper clicks (8–29 kr). Booking and "besiktningsman hus" phrases cost 64–96 kr and 19–79 kr per click.
